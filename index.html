About Rakshak Ganesh Utsav

Rakshak Ganesh Utsav is dedicated to uniting thousands of families under the canopy of devotion, social service, and cultural preservation. For decades, our mandal has championed social harmony, education support for the underprivileged, free medical camps, and blood donation drives during the 10-day divine festival.

In reverence to Mother Earth, Rakshak Ganesh Utsav proudly sculpts 100% Mitti (Clay) Eco-friendly Idols colored only with natural Vedic pigments like Haldi and Kumkum, leading a green revolution in Surat.
